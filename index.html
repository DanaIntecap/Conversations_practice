📍 Aisle and Shelf

"Excuse me, where is the coffee?"
Assistant:
"It is in Aisle 3, on the first shelf."
Customer:
"Thank you! And... is the pasta on sale?"
Assistant:
"Yes, it is. It's only $1 the bag."
