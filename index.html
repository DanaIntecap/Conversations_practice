Unit 4 • CEFR Level: A1.1

AT THE SUPERMARKET

Topic: Shopping, Prices, and Locations

Objective: Practice asking for items, prices, and locations in a supermarket context.
Instructions: Click on the new vocabulary words, the text, or the 🔊 buttons to listen to the correct pronunciation.

📚 New Vocabulary

🔊 📉 discount A reduction in the usual price of an item.
🔊 🏷️ how much A phrase used to ask about the price or quantity.
🔊 🛒 aisle A passage between shelves in a supermarket.
🔊 🥫 shelf A flat surface where products are displayed.
🔊 ⚖️ pound A unit of weight used for food items.
🔊 💵 cash Money in the form of coins or notes.

💰 Checking Prices

Customer:
"What is the price of an apple?"
Assistant:
"The apples are $11.00 per kilo."

📍 Aisle and Shelf

Customer:
"Excuse me, where is the coffee?"
Assistant:
"It is in Aisle 3, on the first shelf."

🛒 Finding Items

Customer:
"Excuse me, where is the milk?"
Assistant:
"It is in the beverages section."

🏷️ Sales and Regular Price

Customer:
"Is the pasta on sale?"
Assistant:
"No, it is at a regular price today."

🏁 Final Challenge: At the Store

Customer:
"Excuse me, where is the chicken?"
Assistant:
"It is in Aisle 4, on the second shelf."
Customer:
"Thanks! And what is the price of the fish?"
Assistant:
"The fish is $10.00. It is on sale!"
Customer:
"Great. Where are the oranges? I want to make fish with orange sauce."
Assistant:
"That sounds delicious. They are in the third aisle."
Assistant:
"Ready?"
Customer:
"Yes, I have everything I need."
Assistant:
"It is a total of $12.50. Cash or credit?"
Customer:
"Cash, thank you!"
Assistant:
"My pleasure, have a nice day!"
Customer:
"Thanks, have a good one!"
